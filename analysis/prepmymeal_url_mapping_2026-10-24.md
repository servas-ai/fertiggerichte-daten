## 1. Executive Summary & Root Cause of Slug Mismatches

PrepMyMeal (`prepmymeal.at`) maintains separate Shopify product records per box-size price tier (`6er-Box` at `10,49 €` / `12,48 € XL` / `12,49 € Athletes`, `12er-Box` at `9,99 €`, `18er-Box` at `9,49 €`, `24er-Box` at `8,99 €`, plus `CH:` CHF records). When PrepMyMeal rotates a dish in the **6er-Box** (`frozen-6er-meal-box` / `ready2go-gerichte-de`), they frequently **overwrite the existing 6er-Box product title, image, SKU, and nutrition in place without changing its Shopify URL handle**, while creating fresh handles (`...-1`, `...-2`, `...-3`) for the 12er/18er/24er-Box discount tiers.

Consequently:
1. **26 active 6er-Box rows (`#2784`, `#2837` Thai Pa Naeng, etc.)** have a legacy predecessor dish name in their URL slug (e.g. `#2837` `Thai Pa Naeng mit Hähnchenbrust` -> `/products/lachs-blumenkohl-grune-bohnen`), **but that URL is the official, active 6er-Box product (`10,49 €`, `available: true`) for the current dish**. Switching `#2837` to `/products/thai-pa-naeng-mit-hahnchenbrust-1` would point to an uncollected 12er-Box tier (`9,99 €`, `collections: []`). **Recommendation:** Keep the active 6er-Box URLs on those 26 rows.
2. **8 rows have real URL, availability, tier, or duplicate defects** that should be fixed in the data pipeline (`#1532, #1533, #1534–#1536, #2785, #2788, #2847`, plus `#2854/#2859` and `#2857/#2845`).
